# MFS (Mully Fulfillment Services) — Brand Voice & Blog Guidelines

## Who We Are
MFS is a premium 3PL (third-party logistics) fulfillment company based in Ohio. We serve high-growth DTC (direct-to-consumer) e-commerce brands — primarily in apparel, supplements, beauty, and lifestyle. We are NOT a massive faceless warehouse. We are a founder-led, hands-on fulfillment partner that treats every brand like our own.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Confident but not arrogant** — We know we're good, but we prove it with results, not hype
- **Direct and clear** — No corporate jargon, no fluff. Say it plainly
- **Operator-to-operator** — We speak like founders talking to founders
- **Subtly premium** — Clean, sharp, modern. Think less "warehouse vibes" and more "operational excellence"
- **Data-backed** — Always anchor claims in real numbers and real results
- **Never salesy** — Educate and build trust. The CTA is implicit, not shoved down throats

## Writing Style Rules
1. Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
2. Use subheadings to break up content
3. Lead with the insight, not the setup
4. Include at least one specific data point or real-world example
5. End with a clear takeaway, not a generic CTA
6. Avoid: "In today's fast-paced world", "At the end of the day", "It's no secret that", "In conclusion"
7. Avoid: exclamation marks, emojis, buzzwords like "synergy", "leverage", "game-changer"
8. Target length: 600-900 words
9. Format: Markdown with H2 subheadings
10. Include a meta description (under 160 characters) at the top

## Target Audience
- DTC brand founders doing $50K-$5M/month in revenue
- E-commerce operators evaluating or switching 3PLs
- Shopify store owners scaling past self-fulfillment
- Marketing managers who care about shipping speed's impact on customer experience

## Topic Categories (rotate through these)
1. **Fulfillment Operations** — Pick/pack efficiency, error reduction, warehouse management
2. **Scaling DTC** — When to outsource fulfillment, growing pains, operational milestones
3. **Shipping & Delivery** — Carrier strategy, shipping speed vs cost, last-mile optimization
4. **Customer Experience** — How fulfillment impacts reviews, retention, and LTV
5. **E-commerce Trends** — Subscription commerce, influencer drops, seasonal prep, peak season
6. **Cost Optimization** — Hidden 3PL fees, fulfillment cost benchmarks, packaging efficiency
7. **Brand Partnerships** — Case study angles, what to look for in a 3PL, red flags
8. **Behind the Scenes at MFS** — Warehouse operations, team culture, how we handle peak volume

## SEO Guidelines
- Target one primary keyword per post (naturally woven in, not stuffed)
- Use the keyword in the title, first paragraph, and at least one subheading
- Include 2-3 related secondary keywords
- Write titles that are specific and benefit-driven (e.g., "Why Your 3PL's Error Rate Is Costing You Customers" not "Fulfillment Tips")
